How The EYES Works
01. Simply Connected
1. One-Click Connection
  • Supported Applications: Works with Gmail, Slack, GitHub, Notion, Discord, and Dropbox.
  • Password-Free Access: Links accounts using safe, industry-standard authentication.
  • Automatic Updates: Syncs new files, tasks, and conversations automatically in the background.
2. Privacy Shielding
  • Exclude Private Accounts: Block personal email domains, specific keywords, or chat channels.
  • Automatic Scrubbing: Masks credit card numbers, passwords, and IDs before indexing.
  • Clean Timelines: Organizes communication events into a unified, private history log.
3. Smart Search & Ask
  • Natural Questions: Search past chats and files using simple queries like "What did I promise to send Sai?".
  • Direct Source Links: Every answer is backed by a direct link to the original email or message.
  • Reliable Facts: The AI only responds using your verified history, eliminating made-up answers.
Conversations With Your Past
02. Smart Search
Find What You Mean, Not Just What You Type
  • Smart Conceptual Search: If you search for "website feedback", it will find chats discussing "page fixes" or "design revisions".
  • Exact Detail Matching: Prioritizes exact terms when looking for specific dates, names, file types, or transaction IDs.
  • Fast Results: Delivers highly accurate, relevant answers from all connected apps in seconds.
Answers You Can Verify and Trust
  • Verifiable Proof: Every single answer includes a direct, clickable link pointing back to the original Slack chat, email, or Notion doc.
  • Factual Boundaries: The AI is locked to only answer using verified documents. If it doesn't know, it tells you honestly.
  • Context-Aware: Remembers your active projects to tailor answers to your current work.
Track Commitments & Promises
03. Reliability Ledger
Automatic Promise Ledger
  • Auto-Logs Deliverables: Detects whenever you or a contact promises a deliverable or schedules a deadline.
  • Categorizes Obligations: Organizes items as active commitments, delayed tasks, or items that were explicitly cancelled.
  • Follow-Through Index: Displays a visual dashboard showing your personal follow-through rates over time.
Calendar Verification
  • Calendar Cross-Check: Automatically scans Google Calendar entries around task due dates.
  • Automatic Status Checks: Marks commitments as completed if a corresponding calendar entry is found, or flags them as pending.
  • Next-Step Action Prompts: Recommends quick follow-up draft messages for overdue promises.

Data Trust & Privacy Policies
05. Security & Onboarding
Privacy First: Your Data, Your Control
  • 1-Click Data Self-Destruct: Permanently delete your profile and erase all indexed memories, linked app credentials, and history logs from our servers. This is completely non-reversible.
  • Automatic Information Scrubbing: Automatically hides sensitive details (like credit card numbers, bank records, and personal passwords) before processing any text.
  • Zero Data Training Guarantees: Your work data is processed using private enterprise connections and is never stored, reviewed, or used to train public AI models.
