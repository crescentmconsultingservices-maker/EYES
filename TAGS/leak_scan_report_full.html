§1

How to read this report

Every claim in this document carries its receipt. Nothing here is generated, estimated from patterns, or inferred from industry averages — each flagged thread cites the exact email it came from, with sender, date, and the line that mattered. If a receipt doesn't convince you, discard the claim. The estimated values use your stated average placement fee of €7,000 and are marked per-thread; they are directional, the threads are not.

Method — one paragraph

EYES connected to the mailbox read-only, reconstructed every conversation thread from the last 182 days, and classified each against four leak patterns: proposals sent and never chased, clients gone quiet after activity, commitments made and not closed, and inbound requests never answered. A thread is flagged only when the evidence clears a 0.80 confidence threshold. 23 of 4,218 threads cleared it.

§4

What the four patterns mean

OPEN_PROPOSAL A quote, proposal, or terms you sent that received no decision and no follow-up from your side.
GHOSTED_CLIENT An active client conversation that stopped without a rejection — silence, not a no.
DROPPED_COMMITMENT Something your side promised in writing, with no evidence it was delivered.
UNANSWERED_INBOUND Money that knocked — an inbound request or warm referral that never got a reply.
