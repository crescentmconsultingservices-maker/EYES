1. **The Shield (Modal GLiNER):** A 1.7GB NLP model running on a scalable cloud container that aggressively filters out 98% of junk data for free. 
2. **The Sniper (Claude Haiku):** A hyper-fast, low-cost LLM wrapped in extremely strict Anti-Hallucination prompts that only processes the 2% of highly relevant data. 

**The Result:** We secured the required intelligence while violently crushing the server costs down from **$25.00+** to just **$0.27 per user**.
