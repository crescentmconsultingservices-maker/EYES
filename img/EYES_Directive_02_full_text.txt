00The Law of Steps — how this document works
One rule governs everything below. Read it first, because it is the entire operating
system of this directive.
This directive is a sequence of locked steps. Each step ends in a gate — a concrete,
checkable end goal with the evidence it requires. The law:
You do not begin Step N+1 until the gate of Step N is met and its
evidence is delivered. No parallel starts on future steps. No "we also
began." The order is the strategy.
This is not bureaucracy, and here is the arithmetic that proves it. The Phase 1 quality gate has now
been attempted four times: first with no numbers, then with placeholder numbers, then with the word
"perfectly" and no memo, and on July 10 with real numbers under an inverted verdict. Those four
attempts consumed three weeks. The honest version of the same gate — a volunteer labeling fifty
of their own emails and one measured run — takes two days. Skipping validation has not made us
fast. It is the single largest source of our slowness. Honesty is the speed strategy, and this directive
is built to prove it: followed exactly, the Chronic layer is live in the product for all alpha users in
roughly ten working days, and organ construction begins in week three.
THE ONE DISTINCTION, RESTATED ONCE, THEN NEVER AGAIN
Wired means connected and running. Validated means what the system says about a person
is true, and we measured it. On this product the distance between those words is the entire
distance between a demo and a business. Every gate below exists to close that distance one
step at a time.
Nothing in this document questions the team's ability — the plumbing built these past three weeks
(Modal deployment, live async batches, decay escalation, the candidate filter, the alias fix) is real
and it stays. What changes today is sequence and proof. Build fast inside each step. Never across
them.

01Two standing changes, effective today
These are not steps. They are permanent changes to how we operate, and they precede
everything.
A · The founder's corpus is retired
The founder's Gmail is disconnected from all test and development pipelines today. It is no longer
the test corpus, the demo corpus, or the debugging corpus — for any purpose, by anyone. Cached
copies, exports, and fixtures derived from it are deleted. Written confirmation of the disconnect and
deletion is the first deliverable of this directive.
Its replacement: one consenting volunteer inside Crescent Moon connects their own real inbox as
the engineering corpus. Requirements: at least one year of history, roughly 2,000+ emails, a
genuine mix of professional and personal mail, and — critically — real commitments, delays, and
decisions in natural language. Someone who coordinates work is ideal.
WHY THIS IS BETTER SCIENCE, NOT JUST BETTER PRIVACY
The volunteer labels their own email. The person who lived the messages knows with
certainty whether "I'll send it Friday" was a real commitment and whether it was kept —
ground truth of a quality no outsider can produce. Privacy discipline rides along: only the
volunteer reads their raw mail; only labels, spans, and aggregate metrics ever leave their
session; no other team member browses the corpus.
B · Verdict authority moves to the founder
From today: the team produces numbers; the founder issues every GO / NO-GO, judged against
thresholds agreed before the run (Section 02). Memos delivered under this directive contain
measurements, breakdowns, and observations — and no verdict line. This is not a demotion; it is
the same separation the product itself is built on: the estimator reports what is true, the decision-
maker decides what to do. Four verdicts have now been issued on invalid data. The fifth verdict will
be issued on valid data, by the founder, and it will hold.
ALSO ABSORBED HERE — THE STANDING RULES THAT REMAIN IN FORCE
Anchoring (every claim carries its receipt to source and span) — unchanged, eternal. Track
One (the live product) remains priority one daily. The daily document (did / finished / doing
tomorrow) continues. Any new always-on service, infrastructure, or per-record LLM cost
requires a one-page cost note before code is written. And loop the founder in before new
costs harden — never after.

02The math — thresholds agreed before any run
These numbers are fixed now, in writing, so no future memo can bend a verdict around
them.
EYES's errors are not symmetric, and the thresholds encode that. A false "you committed
to X" is product-killing — it teaches the user the system invents things about their life. A
miss is merely invisible — the user never knows. Therefore: precision is sacred, recall
can climb. We tune conservative, always.

AND THE DEFINITIONS THE LABELS ARE GRADED AGAINST — LOCKED
commitment = the user's own first-person promise to do a thing ("I'll get it to you Friday,"
"leave it with me"). delayed_on = the user's own commitment with elapsed time and no
action — never a third-party blocker. "The vendor hasn't replied" is blocked_by, a separate
relation, not delayed_on. decided_against = an active, stated rejection. Head
normalization: heads normalize to User only when the subject is first-person in user-authored
text (I / me / my / we-including-user). Named third parties keep their own entity as head —
"Sarah committed to the deployment" is Sarah's commitment, never the user's. In received
mail, the sender's "I" maps to the sender's entity.

DAYS 1–

THE WORK
EXPLICITLY FORBIDDEN IN THIS STEP
Using any model's output as ground truth, in whole or in part. The July 10 memo graded the new
engine against the old model's garbage — misses and hallucinations were uninterpretable even
in principle. Ground truth is human, or it is not ground truth.
GATE 1 — YOU MAY PROCEED WHEN
Definitions confirmed in writing · frozen labeled file delivered with record count, per-type
label counts, and ≥ 15 implicit cases shown.
The team replies to Section 02's definitions with either "agreed" or specific objections.
Silence is not agreement. Once agreed, the definitions are frozen.
The volunteer selects 50 records of their own inbox — stratified, not just sequential: the set
must contain at least 15 implicit commitment-class cases ("I'll get it to you Friday," "leave it
with me," "count on me"), plus real delays and decisions. If a random pull lacks them, swap
records in until it doesn't. An unrepresentative sample is a broken instrument.
The volunteer hand-labels every true relation in those 50 records against the frozen
definitions — head, tail, type, text span. Labels are saved as a frozen JSON ground-truth file,
hash-stamped. After freezing, the file is never edited.

WHAT THE JULY 10 SHAPE ALREADY PREDICTS
Six total extractions from a full run points at a starved fallback — a filter passing near-zero

GATE 3 — TWO EXITS
Pass (all Section 02 thresholds met) →  skip to Step 5. Fail →  Step 4, carrying the memo's
diagnosis. Both exits are legitimate. Only an unverdicted, fully-broken-out memo opens
either one.
Add logging so every run reports: filter pass-rate (records in →  records forwarded to the
LLM), per-record extractor attribution (GLiNER vs fallback, per relation), and per-relation-type
extraction counts.
Publish the current candidate-filter pattern list in the daily doc. Reference set — commitment
signals: "I'll," "I will," "let me," "count on me," "leave it to/with me," "we'll," "I can handle,"
"consider it done," "by [day/time] ." Delay signals: "waiting on/for," "blocked," "stuck," "still
hasn't," "taking longer," "holding up." Decision signals: "pass on," "drop," "kill," "scrap,"
"opted not," "different direction," "not going to."
Verify the fallback prompt contains: the frozen definitions, 3–5 few-shot implicit positive
examples per relation type, negative examples (AI commands, plural business cases, third-
party blockers), and a required output schema of head / tail / type / span / confidence. No

the frozen labels.
The memo reports, per relation type: precision, recall, true-positive counts — plus every miss
with the text span that was missed, every hallucination with what was invented, the filter
pass-rate, and the extractor attribution for each true positive (this split tells us where to tune).
No verdict line in the memo. Numbers only. The founder reads it against Section 02 the same
day and issues the call.

4The tuning loop — only if Gate Run A fails DAYS 3–5
THE WORK — IN THIS ORDER, BECAUSE ERRORS CONCENTRATE
THE THRASH GUARD
Maximum three tuning iterations. If thresholds aren't met after three, stop and escalate to the
founder with findings — the failure mode is then architectural, not parametric, and thrashing
hides that. (The known escape hatch, pre-approved if it comes to it: route a larger share of
candidates to the LLM and accept the bounded cost; the cost note rule applies.)
GATE 4 — YOU MAY PROCEED WHEN
All Section 02 thresholds met on the frozen 50, shown in a numbers-only memo, verdict
issued by the founder.
5Gate Run B — confirmation on fresh, unseen data DAYS 5–6
WHY THIS STEP EXISTS
Three tuning passes against one fixed set risks overfitting to those 50 records. Gate Run B is the
guard: the volunteer labels a fresh 100–150 records the pipeline has never seen during tuning,
same definitions, same stratification (implicit cases included). One run. No tuning after seeing the
results. This is the exam, not the practice.
PASS CONDITION
Precision thresholds hold in full ( ≥ 85 / ≥ 85 / ≥ 80). Recall may sit up to 8 points below the Gate-A
figure without failing — small honest degradation on fresh data is expected; collapse is not.
GATE 5 — THE PHASE 1 GATE, FINALLY CLOSED
Numbers-only memo on the fresh set →  founder issues the GO / NO-GO. A GO here is the
Phase 1 quality gate passed — honestly, on the fifth attempt, on ground that will hold. A
NO-GO returns to Step 4 findings with the founder deciding the architectural response.
Either way: this memo is the one that finally counts.
Filter first. If pass-rate < 15% on a real inbox, widen the pattern list (Step 2 reference set)
until the band is reached. This is the prime suspect.
Fallback prompt second. Misses concentrated in implicit cases →  strengthen the few-shot
implicit examples. Hallucinations →  strengthen the negative examples.
GLiNER threshold third. Tune the local confidence cut only after the two above.
Head normalization — verify the Section 02 rule in code: first-person only, named third
parties keep their heads.
After each change: re-run against the same frozen 50. That is why the file is frozen — same
test, honest comparison.
