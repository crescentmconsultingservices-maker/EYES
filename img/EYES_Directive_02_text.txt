00The Law of Steps — how this document works
One rule governs everything below. Read it first, because it is the entire operating
system of this directive.
This directive is a sequence of locked steps. Each step ends in a gate — a concrete,
checkable end goal with the evidence it requires. The law:
You do not begin Step N+1 until the gate of Step N is met and its
evidence is delivered. No parallel starts on future steps. No "we also
began." The order is the strategy.
This is not bureaucracy, and here is the arithmetic that proves it. The Phase 1 quality gate has now
been attempted four times: first with no numbers, then with placeholder numbers, then with the word
"perfectly" and no memo, and on July 10 with real numbers under an inverted verdict. Those four
attempts consumed three weeks. The honest version of the same gate — a volunteer labeling fifty
of their own emails and one measured run — takes two days. Skipping validation has not made us
fast. It is the single largest source of our slowness. Honesty is the speed strategy, and this directive
is built to prove it: followed exactly, the Chronic layer is live in the product for all alpha users in
roughly ten working days, and organ construction begins in week three.
THE ONE DISTINCTION, RESTATED ONCE, THEN NEVER AGAIN
Wired means connected and running. Validated means what the system says about a person
is true, and we measured it. On this product the distance between those words is the entire
distance between a demo and a business. Every gate below exists to close that distance one
step at a time.
Nothing in this document questions the team's ability — the plumbing built these past three weeks
(Modal deployment, live async batches, decay escalation, the candidate filter, the alias fix) is real
and it stays. What changes today is sequence and proof. Build fast inside each step. Never across
them.
